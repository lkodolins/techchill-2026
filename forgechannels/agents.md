## About ForgeChannels (What We're Building)
ForgeChannels is a new product in the Forge Stack — the first outreach tool that reaches B2B prospects on **chat platforms** (Google Chat, Microsoft Teams) instead of crowded email inboxes.

### Key Value Props
- **3-5x higher response rates** compared to cold email
- **Zero competition** — nobody else is doing outreach via Google Chat or Teams
- **AI-personalized messages** — each message is tailored to the prospect's role, company, and recent activity
- **Multi-channel routing** — automatically detects whether a prospect uses Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 and routes to the right platform
- **Calendar integration** — schedule calls directly from the conversation with Google Meet links
- **Complements Salesforge** — email + LinkedIn via Salesforge, chat channels via ForgeChannels = full coverage

### How It Works
1. Upload a CSV of contacts (or feed from Salesforge/Leadsforge)
2. MX record analysis detects which chat platform each contact uses
3. AI generates a personalized opener for each contact
4. Messages sent via Google Chat API or Microsoft Teams API
5. AI handles the ongoing conversation, steering toward a booked demo call

### Who It's For
- Sales teams already using Salesforge who want to add a new channel
- SDRs and BDRs looking for untapped outreach channels
- Growth teams at SaaS companies tired of 2% email reply rates
- Agencies managing outreach for multiple clients

### The Pitch (Why Chat > Email)
- Email inboxes: saturated, spam-filtered, 2% reply rates
- LinkedIn: everyone's doing it, connection limits, message requests buried
- Google Chat / Teams: near-zero cold outreach today, native to how people work, no promotional tab
- People have Chat/Teams open all day — it's where they actually communicate with colleagues

### Pricing (ForgeChannels)

- Looking for companies willing to try it and give feedback
- No commitment required — just a 15-min demo call to walk through it
- Will integrate into Salesforge plans once launched

### Common Objections & Responses
- **"Isn't this spam?"** → Unlike email spam, chat messages feel personal and direct. We limit volume and personalize every message. Response rates prove people engage.
- **"Will people find this annoying?"** → First messages go to message requests — the recipient has full control. Our data shows people are curious, not annoyed.
- **"We already use email outreach"** → Great — this complements email, it doesn't replace it. Salesforge handles email + LinkedIn, ForgeChannels adds chat. Full coverage.
- **"We already use Salesforge"** → Perfect — ForgeChannels plugs right in. Same contacts, new channel, higher response rates.
- **"How do you know which platform they use?"** → We analyze MX records from their email domain. Google Workspace users get Google Chat, Microsoft 365 users get Teams. Automatic routing.
- **"What about Slack?"** → On the roadmap. Starting with Google Chat and Teams since they map directly to email domains. Slack community outreach is next.
