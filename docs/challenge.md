# Salesforge Challenge — TechChill 2026

## What Should Participants Build?

Design and prototype systems, tools, or experiments that directly increase **qualified demo bookings** across the funnel — from outbound OR inbound to demo attended.

Focus on high-leverage, scalable ideas that can realistically **double pipeline generation**.

---

## Problem Statement

Sales teams today don't struggle with tools — they struggle with **signal, personalization, and conversion at scale**.

**Your mission:** Build a solution(s) that meaningfully increases the number of qualified booked demos without doubling effort or spend.

---

## Example Areas To Explore

### 1. Hyper-Personalized Outbound at Scale

- AI-generated messaging that actually converts
- Dynamic personalization using real-time signals (job changes, funding, intent data)
- Think beyond emails — LinkedIn and cold calling as the usual channels

### 2. Conversion Optimization Engines

- Smart booking flows that increase show rates
- AI SDR assistants that handle objections in real time
- Website or landing page personalization to drive demo intent

### 3. Lead Scoring & Prioritization

- Identify who is most likely to book a demo *now*
- Predictive models using behavioral + firmographic data
- Autonomous pipeline prioritization

### 4. Growth Loops & Virality

- Referral systems for B2B demos
- Incentive mechanisms that turn users into promoters
- Network effects within sales workflows

### 5. AI Agents for Pipeline Generation

Fully or semi-autonomous agents that:
- Prospect
- Reach out
- Follow up
- Book meetings

### 6. Organic Content

- pSEO
- GEO related optimization
- Programmatic YouTube Content
- AI UGC
